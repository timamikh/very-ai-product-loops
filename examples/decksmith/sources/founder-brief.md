# Source — Founder brief (Decksmith)

> The raw idea and the founder's early decisions, captured at kickoff. This is the **origin** the
> concept passport is built from. Fictional sample — no real data. Captured facts carry a tag;
> the founder's own bets are `[assumption]` until evidenced.

## The idea (as told)

"Every AI slide tool today makes you pick one of two bad options: **pretty but locked** — it
exports images or a rigid template you can't really edit — **or editable but ugly** — it dumps
plain text into default PowerPoint styling. I want a generator that outputs **native, fully
editable** `.pptx`/`.key` (real shapes, text, layouts you can edit like your own) that also
**look designed**." [sourced: founder brief]

## What the founder observed

- People who make client-facing decks often (sales, marketing) regenerate AI output by hand
  because it "looks templated" — the tool saves less time than promised. [sourced: founder brief]
- The complaint isn't only visual: decks also come back with the **wrong structure/story** for
  the audience. [sourced: founder brief]
- Editability is table-stakes in that crowd: locked/image output is a non-starter. [sourced: founder brief]

## What we have / can build (honest inventory)

- A prototype rendering approach that emits **native slide objects** (not images) and applies
  layout/spacing rules. Quality-at-scale is unproven. [assumption]
- Access to a small corpus of well-designed decks to learn design patterns from (to be assembled).
  [assumption]
- Founder's own design taste / credibility in the design community. [assumption]

## Early bets the founder stated

- Lead with **salespeople & marketers** — highest frequency × stakes, and usually a company
  budget behind the purchase. [assumption]
- The riskiest thing is **feasibility**: can the engine *reliably* produce files that are both
  genuinely editable and genuinely well-designed, at scale. [assumption]
- Monetization and willingness-to-pay: unknown, deliberately deferred to Strategy. [assumption]

## Explicitly out of scope (for now)

- Real-time collaboration / co-editing — not part of the concept bet.
- Non-slide formats (docs, PDFs as primary output).
- Pricing — deferred to Step 3 (Strategy).

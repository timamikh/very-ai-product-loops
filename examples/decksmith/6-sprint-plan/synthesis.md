# synthesis — the working (Sprint 1)

_Source of truth for the two **synthesis** sections of `6-sprint-plan.md`: `#sprint-goal` and
`#delivery`. Not a library method — a synthesis of the ranked must-set (`prioritization-sprint-plan`)
against the period gate (`5#period-goals`). Holds the reasoning the two thin sections project._

## 1 · Sprint goal (→ `#sprint-goal`)

**The gate this sprint serves.** Period-1 gate = (A) an export engine passing the design-acceptance
eval (`H-001`) + (B) first S1 signals (`H-011`/`H-010`). A ~2-week sprint cannot *finish* (A) or read
(B) — so Sprint 1's honest job is to make the bet **testable**:

- **Start (A):** ship the first slice of the native-export engine (F-1) **and** the acceptance
  instrumentation (F-2) — after this sprint `H-001` is *measurable*, which it is not today (`R-012`).
- **Start (B):** launch founder-community recruitment (A-1) with a surface to capture it (T-1) — the
  `H-011` clock starts, partners accrue toward the ≥8 target.

At `concept-viability` the sprint goal is **evidence-readiness, not traction** — the status's learning
goal (`statuses/`). Stated in one line in the artifact; the reasoning is here.

## 2 · Delivery (→ `#delivery`)

**What is handed off, and how results return.**

- **Handed off** to the team's own development/execution flow: F-1, F-2 (dev board), A-1 (founder),
  T-1 (back-office) — the four musts. The framework ends at this handoff; grooming to sub-tasks happens
  downstream, below the framework's feature/activity/task altitude.
- **Backlog** (F-3/A-2/F-4/T-2) is groomed next, in rank order.
- **Results flow back up — the cascade's return path:** F-1+F-2 landing makes `H-001` readable (the
  eval reads out next sprint via F-3); A-1+T-1 produce the `H-011`/`M-activated` read. A **missed
  `M-…` or refuted `H-…` bubbles to `5-tactical-plan.md`**, and further up if a strategic bet is hit:
  `H-001` refuted → revisit `3#how-to-win`; `H-011` refuted → `R-008` (validate a 2nd channel);
  `H-010` proxy fails → `R-011` (WTP). The upward revisit is triggered **by name**, not by vibes.

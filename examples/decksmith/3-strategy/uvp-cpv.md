# UVP & CPV — the working

_Source of truth for `3-strategy.md#uvp-cpv`. Lens: Dunford positioning (value is relative to a
chosen *alternative*, for a *best-fit* customer) + Value Proposition Canvas + customer-perceived
value. The lead segment's value is worked **per situation** before the one-liner — a CVP written
above the situations is a slogan._

## 1 · Best-fit customer

Within S1: the **client-facing deck-maker** at an agency/consultancy or in-house sales/marketing
who must produce a **polished, on-brand deck** *and* **hand over / keep editing a real editable
file**. The two requirements bind at once only for this customer — which is why our value is most
obvious to them. Sharpest instance: the design-native agency/consultant.

## 2 · The named alternative (value is relative to it)

What they do **today**:
- **(a)** Generate in **Gamma/Canva** for looks, then **rebuild by hand in PowerPoint** to get an
  editable, on-brand file — the "restyle tax" (`H-003`).
- **(b)** Start in **PowerPoint/templates** and spend hours designing from scratch.
- **(c)** Use **Plus AI / Copilot** and get a native but generic deck they restyle anyway.

The primary alternative we position against is **(a) Gamma/Canva + manual rebuild** — the polished-
but-locked path S1 actually uses.

## 3 · Lead segment worked per situation (situation · pain · cost of inaction · CVP)

| Situation (trigger) | Pain | Cost of inaction | CVP (outcome in *this* situation) | Signal |
|---------------------|------|------------------|-----------------------------------|--------|
| **New client pitch deck from a brief** | Must look designed *and* be an editable/on-brand file; hours in Gamma then rebuild in PPT | Lost billable hours; a weaker or late pitch | A client-ready, on-brand, **fully-editable** deck in minutes — no rebuild | [assumption] |
| **Recurring client report / QBR deck** | Same restyle done every cycle; repetitive drudgery | Margin bleed on a repeating job | Regenerate on brand, **edit natively**, reuse the brand kit — the tax paid once | [assumption] |
| **Handoff to a client who edits it themselves** | Locked/lossy export from design tools breaks when the client opens it in their own PowerPoint/Keynote | Broken file at the worst moment; rework; reputation hit | A **true native file** the client edits without breakage | [assumption] |

Every CVP is `[assumption]`: Decksmith is a fictional sample with no pilot behind it. The *pains* are
`[sourced: founder brief; 1-concept/segment-pains.md]`; the *value delivered* is unproven — the
honest split.

## 4 · Value vs the alternative (Value Proposition Canvas)

- **Pain relievers:** eliminates the restyle tax (design + native-editable in one pass); no
  export breakage; brand consistency without manual policing.
- **Gain creators:** faster turnaround per client deck; more billable capacity; on-brand by default.
- **Against the alternative (Gamma/Canva + rebuild):** they give looks then take back editability;
  we give both at once. Against Plus AI/Copilot: they give a native file then take back the design.

## 5 · Customer-perceived value (in their words) + signal

How S1 would phrase it: *"I get a deck that already looks like we designed it — and it's a real
PowerPoint I can actually edit and send, so I'm not rebuilding it at midnight."* Signal behind it:
**none yet** — no pilot, no money-behind-words. Tagged `[assumption]`. Discovery interviews +
willingness-to-pay evidence are the first thing the tactical/sprint loop should buy (`money beats
words`).

## 6 · Positioning one-liner (compresses the situations above)

> **For sales & marketing teams who must send client-ready decks, Decksmith generates a fully-
> editable, on-brand PowerPoint or Keynote in minutes — unlike Gamma or Canva, whose polished decks
> lock you into their editor and break on export, because our curated design engine writes native
> files, not screenshots.**

Read against each situation in §3 it holds (pitch / recurring / handoff all turn on
editable-AND-designed). [assumption]

## 7 · Seeded hypotheses (→ register)

- The switching value (S1 abandons "Gamma + rebuild" for the editable-AND-designed promise) rests on

  re-minted**; it is sharpened as the desirability switching bet in `bets.md`.
- The **pricing** claim (S1 pays a premium for this value) is genuinely new → seeded as `H-010` in

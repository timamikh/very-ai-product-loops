# Worklog — where-to-play-how-to-win (fills 3 sections)

Opinionated: Playing to Win cascade. Inputs: passport, analysis (native-fidelity wedge + closing-incumbent
threat). value-definition is revisited here for the moat in how-to-win.

## Winning aspiration

Own **"the AI deck you don't have to rebuild"** for salespeople & marketers — become their default for
client-facing decks that are *both* editable and designed, and reach a defensible position in the lead segment
before incumbents close the native-fidelity gap (⚙️ horizon ~18–24 mo). Not "be a player" — a specific segment,
a specific result. [assumption]

## ≥3 candidate cascades (whole cascades; rejects with why)

- **A — Beachhead wedge (CHOSEN).** WTP: sales/marketing client-deck makers, NA+EU, standalone web app →
  native .pptx/.key. HTW: only tool that is *actually-editable* AND *designed*, out-executing incumbents on the
  fidelity engine; PLG via design/sales communities. Internally consistent: thin moat compensated by focus +
  speed on the exact gap the leader has.
- **B — Horizontal "AI decks for everyone" (rejected).** WTP: all deck-makers. HTW: win on breadth/brand.
  **Loses:** head-on with Gamma/Canva on distribution we don't have and a moat we don't have. No exclusions = no
  choice.
- **C — Enterprise brand-compliance (rejected, kept as expansion).** WTP: enterprise brand/marketing teams. HTW:
  sell on-brand-at-scale + governance. **Loses now:** Copilot owns the M365/enterprise relationship; sales cycle
  too slow for a startup wedge. Revisit as expansion once the beachhead holds.
- **D — API/embed fidelity engine (rejected, kept as expansion).** WTP: other tools (B2B2C) needing native
  export. HTW: "the engine is the moat." **Loses now:** defers demand validation and revenue; needs the engine
  proven first. A Step-3+ option once H-001 (feasibility) is green.

## Where to play (exclusions explicit)

- **Chosen arena:** sales/marketing client-deck makers, NA+EU, standalone web app → native .pptx/.key.
- **Excluded (why):** enterprise brand-compliance (Copilot's turf, slow — deferred to expansion); real-time
  co-editing (out of concept); non-slide formats; education/personal (low WTP); API/embed (later); geographies
  outside NA+EU at first (localization cost, focus).

## How to win (logic + named moats)

- **Logic:** win the native-fidelity wedge — be the only tool whose output is *actually editable AND designed* —
  and out-run incumbents closing the gap by focusing narrowly on the fidelity+design engine, not the app.
- **Moats leveraged (from value-definition, revisited):** execution speed on the fidelity engine (`H-009`),
  design taste + curated corpus (`H-005`). **NOT distribution** — we don't have it; speed + focus substitute.
  Honest: the moat is thin and temporal → the plan is win-fast-then-build-derivative-lock-in (brand kits once
  customers exist).
- **Integration check:** does HTW win in this WTP under this aspiration? Yes *iff* the engine holds quality at
  scale (`H-001`) and we out-execute before incumbents close the gap — both explicit bets, not assumed.

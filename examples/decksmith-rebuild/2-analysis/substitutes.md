# Worklog — substitutes (fills `#substitutes`)

Framed by the **job** (`../1-passport.md#jtbd`), not the category. Baseline three + ≥2 adjacent.

## Map

| Substitute | How it does the job today | Why the customer stays / when it wins |
|------------|---------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| **Do nothing** | reuse an old deck, or send a rough one | wins for low-stakes/internal decks; the deck is "good enough". [assumption] |
| **Do it manually** | build from scratch in PowerPoint/Keynote, or from a company template | wins when brand control is paramount; **the habit force** — the known-good path. [sourced: founder brief] |
| **Self-build with a general LLM** | prompt ChatGPT/Claude for content + hand-format in PPT | wins for *capable* users; **caps WTP for "just generate slides"**. [sourced: market-research] → `R-003` |
| Designer / agency (adjacent) | hire it out | wins for the highest-stakes decks (board, fundraise). [assumption] |
| Template marketplaces / Canva templates (adjacent) | buy a pre-made template, fill it in | wins when a template + manual fill beats generation. [sourced: market-research] |

## Self-build threshold

"Just build it ourselves / with an LLM" beats buying **once the user is comfortable prompting an LLM and
hand-formatting** — i.e. exactly the more technical slice of the lead segment. This caps the ceiling for a
"generation-only" pitch and pushes the value toward *native fidelity + design*, which self-build does worst.
[sourced: market-research] → `R-003`

## Switching friction (what keeps them on the substitute)

Habit (start-from-last-deck), brand-template sunk cost, and trust for client-facing work. Our offer must beat
all three to displace "do it manually", the strongest substitute.

## Seeds

- Strong substitute promoted → `R-003` (self-build caps WTP). "Do it manually" is the incumbent habit — the
  push/pull battle, tracked via H-002/H-006, not a separate risk.

# Examples

Synthetic, fictional walkthroughs of the framework. **No real product or company data** — these
exist to show what a finished artifact looks like and to serve as a reference when running the
[operating loop](../process/OPERATING-LOOP.md). Real product runs stay in private instances.

> **Examples omit the `## Change log` section by design.** A real artifact keeps a dated change log
> (see [`process/CONVENTIONS.md`](../process/CONVENTIONS.md)); the examples drop it so the reference
> shows the clean, final shape of each artifact rather than the history of how it was authored.

| Example | Product (fictional) | Covers |
|---------|---------------------|--------|
| [decksmith](decksmith/) | AI generator of editable, well-designed `.pptx`/`.key` decks | Steps 1–6 (Idea → Sprint Plan) · status `concept-viability` · full instance (config · sources · registers) · a rendered [deliverable](decksmith/deliverables/) (`to-deck` → HTML concept-pitch) |

# very-ai-product-loops

A product-agnostic workflow that takes a product from **idea → sprint plan** through
**nested, gated loops**. Sibling to [`very-ai-framework`](https://github.com/virrius/very-ai-framework):
that one runs the *development* loop (idea → production); this one runs the *product* loops
that sit above it and feed it.

> The agent prepares every artifact from real sources; the human decides at the forks;
> the loops refresh at different speeds and feed each other in both directions.

## The six steps

| # | Step | Horizon | Output |
|---|------|---------|--------|
| 1 | Idea / Concept | product lifetime | `passport.md` |
| 2 | Analysis | 6–12 mo | `analysis.md` |
| 3 | Strategy | 3–12 mo | `strategy.md` |
| 4 | Strategic Plan | 3–12 mo | `strategic-plan.md` |
| 5 | Tactical Plan | 1–3 mo | `tactical-plan.md` |
| 6 | Sprint Plan | 1–2 wk | `sprint-plan.md` |

See [`process/OVERVIEW.md`](process/OVERVIEW.md) for the full model: the loop mechanics,
the three vertical registers (metrics · hypotheses · risks), and the cross-cutting
conventions.

# Status: concept-viability

The earliest stage. There is no product in market and often no users yet. The goal is a
prototype or MVP that tests two things: **can this be built**, and **is there some demand**
worth pursuing toward PMF.

Because there is no product/usage data, this stage's priority goals lean **technical and
discovery** (build, test, learn) — not product/growth metrics. That is expressed by the
`priority_goals` above, not a fixed flag; another product may weight it differently.

**Effect on the loops:** at the tactical step (5) the period goals are about building and
getting signal; the metric tree (4) may be sparse or hypothesis-shaped; the hypothesis
register is the center of gravity.

How it's built · fixed core + pluggable parts

Pluggable tool-skills — swapped per company

The rules of the game stay fixed; methods and output formats plug in and swap — without forking the framework.

Deliverables · adapters assemble from the working area

.xlsxTable — registers & plans for work and sign-off
.docxDocument — a brief or memo for a colleague / lead
PDFDeck — a presentation for a meeting or chat
Same data, different formats. You never re-assemble by hand.

# Five Forces, light

The checklist behind `{#niche-risks}` at Step 2. A **synthesis**, not a method: every force is
read from evidence a Step-2 (or Step-3) method already produced — look up, don't research. Two
questions per force; no evidence yet → `— to clarify —` naming the section that will carry it. A
force that reads high seeds one `R-…`.

| Force | Two questions | Where the evidence already sits |
|---|---|---|
| **Buyer power** | Can the buyer walk to a cheaper equivalent within a quarter? Is the price spread across players narrow (a commodity signal)? | `{#competitor-pricing}` — the comparable range at the reference configuration; `{#substitutes}` — do-nothing / manual as the fallback |
| **Rivalry** | Are players trading share for margin (free tiers, discounting)? Is anyone accelerating into our segment? | `{#competitor-strategy}` — the game each plays, with the move that shows it; `{#competitor-dynamics}` — trend per player |
| **Entry barriers** | What must an entrant hold that an LLM rebuild cannot supply? Which claimed moat would an entrant also have? | `{#value-defensibility}` — base moats, killed feature-moats, the taxonomy's counter-positioning row |
| **Substitution** | Which substitute wins in a real segment, at what trigger? Where is the self-build crossover? | `{#substitutes}` — when-it-wins per row, the self-build Fermi, the promoted `R-…` |
| **Supplier power** | Which single-vendor dependency has no tested fallback? What share of COGS sits with one provider? | Step 3 `{#architecture}` — the LLM / infra dependencies `architecture-c4` lists; until then `— to clarify —` |

**Reading the result.** Score each force H/M/L in `2-analysis/synthesis.md` with the section it was
read from; the face is the one force that most caps price or adoption. Never re-derive a number
here — its home is the section that produced it.

**Anti-patterns.** Porter prose with no section reference · a force scored from an industry article
rather than the players in `{#competitors}` · supplier power scored before Step 3 lists dependencies.

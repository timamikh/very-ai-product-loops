_**Play order** is the sequence the bets are played in — an integer, stated for every carried bet,
with the dependency that forces it (bet 2 needs bet 1's evidence · bet 3 needs the channel live). A
wager that cannot be sequenced is a wish, and "all at once" is a resourcing claim nobody costed._

_The `ID` column **is** the register id — reconciled first: a bet restating a hypothesis already
seeded (e.g. a `where-to-play-how-to-win` cascade assumption, or a Step-1 segment/job claim) reuses
and sharpens that `H-…`; no duplicate id for the same claim._
